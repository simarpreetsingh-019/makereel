How it works

  1. Find the stream. makereel asks YouTube, Twitch or Kick where the live video is, the same way a player does.
  2. Record lightly. It copies the stream without re-encoding (almost no CPU) into a rolling 10-minute buffer, and listens to the loudness and chat every second.
  3. Spot the moment. When loudness or chat jumps, a moment opens and stays open while it lasts: a quick reaction or a three-minute bit.
  4. Make the reel. Captions word by word, checked by a second model; framing that follows the speaker or splits webcam and game; your brand and the creator's badge.
  5. Ready to post. 9:16 (or 4:5, 1:1, 16:9) with post text crediting the creator. Edit anything first in the built-in editor.

Questions

Is makereel free?

Yes. It runs on your own computer with nothing to pay for. AI features are optional and use your own key or a local model.

Does it upload my videos?

No. Everything is processed on your computer. It only downloads the stream or video, checks channels and checks for updates.

Which platforms?

YouTube, Twitch and Kick, any site yt-dlp supports, and local video files.

Does it support Hindi and Hinglish?

Yes: language per sentence, a Hinglish speech model, YouTube's own captions for finished videos, and a second model that double-checks.

Do I need a GPU?

No. An NVIDIA GPU makes speech faster; Apple Silicon Macs run well on the CPU.

Can I repost other people's streams?

Credit is not permission. You're responsible for having the right to repost what you clip; check each platform's rules and the creator's wishes.
